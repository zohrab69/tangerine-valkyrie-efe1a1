# JARVIS screen voice (Voice v1)
Team: JARVIS (CEO) · Runs: when you talk or type on the JARVIS screen

- Receives your words from the password-protected screen.
- Reads the last 6 memory entries and the conversation so far, so it knows what happened and understands follow-ups.
- Answers out loud (Kokoro voice, Adam) and on screen; briefing when you greet it.
- Anything that needs approval is sent to your Telegram with buttons.
