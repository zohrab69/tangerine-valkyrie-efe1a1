# Check my product idea (Idea v1)
Team: Analysis / store · Runs: when you send a photo, a link or "check <product>" on Telegram, or ask JARVIS to check one

1. Works out what the product is (the free AI can read photos) and applies your rules: no batteries or chargers, no beauty, kids, food or medical; ⚠️ certification flags.
2. Finds the best 3 CJ suppliers, Canada and US warehouses first, with real shipping quotes to Canada.
3. Market: Google Shopping Canada prices (Amazon.ca and other shops when listed) and Google searches in Canada over 3 months. 2 free SerpApi searches, cached 10 days. TikTok Shop: no official data.
4. Report: verdict, supplier cost, shipping, fees, suggested price, profit per sale, marketing estimate (videos free, small ad test only if you approve), Amazon FBA stock estimate, risks.
5. Approve → product created on Shopify (French title, FR+EN description, photos, price, collection), published, live link sent. You connect it in the CJ app (1 minute).
6. Video team: 3 short vertical videos with different hooks, JARVIS's voice and captions, sent on Telegram with captions and hashtags for you to post.
7. Amazon: prepared only when your seller account and SP-API are connected, with a separate approval before buying stock.
